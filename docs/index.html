Give a date and a sail window.

    The page reads the current from NOAA harmonic station NYH1928, Hudson River at Pier 92, 6 ft bin. This is the nearest current station to Pier 66, about 1.3 nm up-river. The mean flood sets 026°. The mean ebb sets 212°. It reads the tide from NOAA station 8518750, The Battery. It reads the wind from the National Weather Service, gridpoint OKX 33,44. The NWS forecast reaches about 7 days ahead. The NOAA predictions reach much further.

    The tide and the current are astronomical predictions. Wind, rain and river run-off change the real water. On the Hudson the ebb is stronger and longer than the flood. The wind is a forecast, and a forecast is not a measurement. Use each number as a start point, not as a promise. Take the conditions and the go or no-go decision from the dock.
